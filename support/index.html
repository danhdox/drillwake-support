Support

Practical help for the offline iPhone game.

Before reporting a problem

  • Close Drillwake completely, then reopen it.
  • Check that your iPhone is running the latest available iOS update.
  • Confirm that enough free storage remains on the device.

Useful details

If you need help, note your iPhone model, iOS version, Drillwake version, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot or recording can help explain a visual problem.

Never include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Saved games

Progress and settings remain on your device. Starting a new game replaces the current save. Removing Drillwake also removes its locally stored data.

Private contact form

A privacy-safe support form is being prepared. It will appear here once its delivery service is configured. No personal address or developer profile is published on this site.
