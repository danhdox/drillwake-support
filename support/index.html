Support Privacy

DRILLWAKE for iPhone

Need a repair crew?

Report a problem through the public support tracker. No account, game login, or purchase information is required to play DRILLWAKE.

What to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The DRILLWAKE version shown in App Store Connect.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording when useful.

Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Open a support request

Saved games

Progress is stored only on your device. Starting a new game replaces the current save, and uninstalling DRILLWAKE removes its locally stored data.
