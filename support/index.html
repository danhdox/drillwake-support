Support

Practical help for the offline game on iPhone, and native iPad support from version 1.10.0.

Before reporting a problem

  • Close Drillwake completely, then reopen it.
  • Check that your iPhone or iPad is running the latest compatible iOS or iPadOS update.
  • Confirm that enough free storage remains on the device.

Useful details

If you need help, note your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, Drillwake version, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot or recording can help explain a visual problem.

Never include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Saved games

Progress and settings remain on your device. Starting a new game replaces the current save. Removing Drillwake also removes its locally stored data.

Optional play statistics

From version 1.10.0, you can help us understand where players get stuck by choosing “Share stats.” This is optional. To stop, open Settings and turn “Share anonymous stats” off. This deletes the on-device statistics identifier and unsent queue; gameplay continues normally. See the privacy policy for details.

Private contact form

A privacy-safe support form is being prepared. It will appear here once its delivery service is configured. No personal address or developer profile is published on this site.
