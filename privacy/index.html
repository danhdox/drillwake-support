Privacy policy

DRILLWAKE is an offline game. It does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Data stored on your device

DRILLWAKE stores game progress and settings locally on your device using Apple UserDefaults. DRILLWAKE does not transmit this information. Starting a new game replaces the current saved game. Uninstalling DRILLWAKE removes its locally stored data.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

DRILLWAKE contains no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking technology, external game servers, or third-party sign-in services.

Children

Because DRILLWAKE does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If DRILLWAKE's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant app version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the DRILLWAKE support tracker.
